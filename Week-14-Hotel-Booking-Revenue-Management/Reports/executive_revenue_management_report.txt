8. REVENUE MANAGEMENT RECOMMENDATIONS
Pricing Optimization: Review monthly arrival demand and ADR together. Consider higher rates during consistently high-demand periods and suitable promotions during lower-demand periods.
Hotel-Specific Pricing: Develop separate pricing strategies for City Hotel and Resort Hotel because their demand and cancellation patterns may differ.
Cancellation Prevention: Review the market segments with higher cancellation rates. Consider suitable deposit policies, clear cancellation terms, and booking confirmation reminders.
Lead-Time Monitoring: Monitor bookings made far in advance when their observed cancellation rate is high. Use reminders and appropriate confirmation procedures.
Seasonal Planning: Use monthly arrival trends to plan staffing, room preparation, supplies, and promotional campaigns.
Overbooking Protection: Do not overbook using the overall cancellation rate alone. Consider hotel type, arrival date, market segment, lead time, room availability, and expected no-shows. Apply any overbooking limit only after checking historical patterns and current room inventory.

9. ANALYSIS LIMITATIONS
Estimated revenue is calculated as ADR multiplied by total nights for completed stays. It is not actual accounting revenue.
The dataset does not provide total available room inventory, so occupancy rate and RevPAR cannot be calculated reliably.
The analysis identifies historical relationships and does not guarantee future demand or cancellation behavior.
